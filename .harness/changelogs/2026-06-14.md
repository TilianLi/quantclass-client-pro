## bootstrap-harness (general agent)

Created `.harness/` for QuantclassClient on the
`feature/mcp-support` worktree. 7 agents (1 orchestrator + 6 reins),
4 docs under `.harness/docs/`. No code changes — the bootstrap is
documentation and routing only.

Roster: `developer`, `tester`, `code-reviewer`, `electron-expert`,
`renderer-expert`, `mcp-expert`. The MCP specialist is the only one
that's strictly required for the in-flight feature; the other two
specialists (electron / renderer) are justified by the size of
their respective subsystems.

See `.harness/BOOTSTRAP_REPORT.md` for the full design rationale
and the user-review checklist.
